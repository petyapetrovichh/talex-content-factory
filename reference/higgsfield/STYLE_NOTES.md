# Higgsfield "$1B" milestone video — style notes (reference for TaleX videos)

Source: Higgsfield X post, Sep 24 2026. 26s, 24fps, 1440x1080 (4:3), ~27 cuts.
This is 2D/2.5D motion design (not AI-generated footage). Use it as the quality bar.

## Techniques observed (frame-by-frame)
- Counter opening (0–3s): neon lime digits with strong bloom/glow on black, subtle radial
  vignette. Camera pans horizontally along a long number with heavy directional motion blur.
  Color flashes between accent colors (pink -> yellow -> lime) during fast moves = chromatic
  aberration / RGB split on motion.
- Whip transition to white: horizontal speed streaks (thin colored bars), then the number
  lands in a rounded pill badge.
- Pill badge: thin black outline, rounded ends, label sitting ON the top border (logo + name),
  caption in bold caps under the pill. On entry the outline has colored "echo" trails
  (2–3 offset copies in accent colors) that collapse into one clean stroke.
- Text reveal: letters resolve in a scrambled/glitch order ("Higgs i" -> "Higgsfield").
- Transition out: soft radial blur flash in accent color (pink bloom) wiping the frame.
- Rhythm: each fact holds 1–2s; montage sections cut every 2–3 frames.
- Background alternates black / white as punctuation between facts.
- One heavy grotesk typeface, tabular numbers, bold caps captions. One main accent (lime)
  + one secondary accent (pink) used only in motion (streaks, echoes, flashes).
- Charts in 3D perspective tilt with depth-of-field blur, then flatten to 2D.
- Real material mixed in: team photos, press headline screenshots with the brand word
  highlighted by a lime marker.
- Music: dense driving electronic track, no quiet parts, cuts land on hits; hard stop at end.

## Translation to TaleX
- Main accent: TaleX green #6FF000 (replaces Higgsfield lime). Black #000 / white #FFF.
- Secondary accent for motion only (streaks/echoes/flashes): pick one — suggestion cyan
  #00E5FF or violet #7B61FF (the FDV line color on the Treasury page).
- Numbers: heavy grotesk or the Treasury page monospace, always tabular-nums.
